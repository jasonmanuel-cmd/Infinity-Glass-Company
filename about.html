About Infinity Glass Company

San Antonio's locally owned and operated residential and commercial glass experts.

Who We Are

San Antonio's Locally Owned Glass Experts

Infinity Glass Company (also known as Infinity Glass & Mirror) is a locally owned and operated glass company based in San Antonio, Texas. We provide expert residential and commercial glass solutions designed to elevate both form and function for homeowners and businesses throughout the greater San Antonio area.

From glass replacement and custom design to brand-new installations, our team delivers precision craftsmanship, high-quality materials, and dependable service on every project. As a locally owned company, we understand the importance of trust and accountability — we treat every home and business like our own.

Infinity Glass Company is located at 10921 Iota Dr, San Antonio, TX 78217, and serves communities across the region including Alamo Heights, Stone Oak, Castle Hills, Shavano Park, Helotes, and beyond.

What We Do

Residential & Commercial Glass Services

Infinity Glass Company specializes exclusively in architectural glass and mirrors for homes and businesses. We do not provide auto glass services.

Window Glass Replacement

Cracked, foggy, or broken windows? We repair and replace residential and commercial window glass using high-quality materials built to last.

Shower Doors & Enclosures

Custom frameless and semi-frameless shower glass with precise measurements, durable hardware, and a clean, modern look.

Storefront Glass

Professional storefront glass installation and repair for retail spaces, offices, and commercial buildings throughout San Antonio.

Custom Mirrors

Bathroom mirrors, closet mirrors, full-wall mirrors, and decorative accents — cut to exact specifications for a seamless finish.

Office Glass Partitions

Modern glass walls and partitions for conference rooms, private offices, and collaborative spaces.

Emergency Glass Repair

Broken window or shattered storefront? We offer fast emergency glass repair with same-day service available.

Important: Infinity Glass Company does NOT provide auto glass services.

We specialize exclusively in architectural glass and mirrors for residential and commercial properties — including windows, shower doors, storefronts, mirrors, partitions, and emergency glass repair.
