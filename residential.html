About Our Residential Services

Severe storms roll through Bexar County with hail and high winds that crack and shatter windows without warning. And the combination of heat and humidity in our climate puts extra stress on shower enclosures and bathroom glass, accelerating wear on seals, hinges, and hardware.

Infinity Glass Company was built to solve these problems for San Antonio homeowners. We are a locally owned and operated glass company based on Iota Drive, right here in the city. We are not a national chain or a franchise that flies in installers from out of state. Our team lives and works in San Antonio, which means we understand the building codes, the climate conditions, and the housing styles that make this city unique. Whether you own a mid-century ranch in Alamo Heights, a new-build in Stone Oak, a historic bungalow near King William, or a sprawling home in the Dominion, we have worked on homes just like yours.

Window Glass Replacement That Makes a Real Difference

When a window breaks, it is more than a cosmetic issue. A cracked or shattered window compromises your home's security, lets in moisture and pests, and allows conditioned air to escape. In San Antonio, where summer temperatures regularly exceed 100 degrees, a broken window can also send your electricity bill through the roof as your AC system works overtime to compensate.

We replace all types of residential window glass, including single-pane windows common in older San Antonio homes, insulated double-pane units found in most modern construction, tempered safety glass required near bathrooms, doors, and low-to-ground windows per the Texas Residential Code, and low-E coated glass that reflects infrared heat to keep your home cooler without overworking your HVAC system.

Many San Antonio homeowners call us about foggy double-pane windows. That cloudy or hazy appearance between the glass panes is not dirt that can be cleaned. It is moisture that has entered the insulated glass unit because the factory seal has failed. This is extremely common in our climate because of the temperature swings between our hot summers and mild but variable winters. The good news is that we can often replace just the glass unit without tearing out the entire window frame, which saves you hundreds of dollars compared to a full window replacement.

Shower Doors and Enclosures Built for Texas Conditions

Your bathroom gets more daily use than almost any other space in your home, and the glass in your shower takes a beating. Hard water deposits from San Antonio's mineral-rich water leave spots and buildup on glass. High humidity promotes mold and mildew around seals. And the daily expansion and contraction caused by hot showers followed by cooler ambient temperatures loosens hardware over time.

We design and install custom shower enclosures that are built to handle these conditions. Our frameless shower doors use heavy-duty tempered glass and premium-grade stainless steel or brass hardware that resists corrosion. Semi-frameless designs offer a balance of clean aesthetics and structural reinforcement. And for homeowners who prefer a more traditional look, our framed enclosures provide maximum durability with a wide range of finish options to match your bathroom fixtures.

Every shower enclosure we install starts with a precise on-site measurement. We do not rely on rough dimensions from a builder's plans or measurements you take yourself. Our technicians visit your home, measure every angle and surface condition of your shower or tub area, and then fabricate the glass panels in our shop to exact specifications. This is how we ensure a perfect fit with tight, clean lines and proper water containment.

Custom Mirrors That Transform Your Space

Mirrors do more than let you check your reflection. A well-placed mirror can make a small room feel larger, bounce natural light deeper into your home, and serve as a design focal point that elevates the entire space. We fabricate and install a wide variety of custom mirrors for San Antonio homes, from practical bathroom vanity mirrors to dramatic floor-to-ceiling accent walls.

Our most popular residential mirror projects include frameless bathroom vanity mirrors with polished or beveled edges, full-length closet door mirrors that make getting ready easier, gym and home office mirrors installed to exact specifications, and decorative mirrored walls for bedrooms, dining rooms, and entryways. Every mirror is cut to your exact dimensions, and we offer clear, grey, antique, and smoked glass options depending on the look you want.

Sliding Glass Door Repair and Replacement

Sliding glass doors are one of the most heavily used features in San Antonio homes. They connect your living space to the backyard, let in natural light, and provide ventilation when the weather is pleasant. But years of daily use in a hot, dusty climate take a toll on the rollers, tracks, and glass panels.

If your sliding door sticks, grinds when you try to open it, has come off the track entirely, or has a cracked or broken glass panel, we can help. We repair and replace the glass, rollers, tracks, weather stripping, and lock mechanisms for all major sliding door brands. In many cases, a worn-out sliding door does not need to be replaced entirely. A new glass panel combined with fresh rollers and a cleaned, lubricated track can make a 20-year-old door slide like new.
