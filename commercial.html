Storefront Glass Installation and Repair

Your storefront is the face of your business. A clean, well-maintained glass facade draws customers in and communicates professionalism. We install and repair all types of commercial storefront systems, including aluminum-framed, frameless, and structural glass curtain walls. Our team works with clear, tinted, frosted, and low-E glass to match your brand's aesthetic while improving energy efficiency. If your storefront glass has been damaged by weather, accidents, or break-ins, we respond quickly to restore your building's appearance and security. We handle everything from single-pane replacements to full storefront rebuilds, coordinating with your schedule to minimize disruption to your business operations.

Office Glass Partitions and Interior Walls

Modern offices thrive on natural light and open sightlines. Glass partitions achieve both while maintaining the privacy and acoustics your team needs. We design and install interior glass walls for conference rooms, executive offices, reception areas, and collaborative workspaces. Options include clear, frosted, switchable privacy glass, and custom-printed designs. Our partition systems are built with tempered or laminated safety glass and precision-engineered hardware for smooth operation and lasting durability. We work closely with architects, designers, and contractors to ensure every glass partition integrates seamlessly with your office layout and design vision.

Security Glass for Commercial Properties

Security is a top concern for businesses in San Antonio. We install a range of security glass solutions designed to protect your property, inventory, and people. Laminated security glass holds together when shattered, deterring break-ins and reducing injury risk. Tempered safety glass is up to four times stronger than standard glass and breaks into small, rounded pieces. For high-security applications, we offer polycarbonate panels and ballistic-rated glass that provide maximum protection without sacrificing visibility. Our team can assess your property's security needs and recommend the right glass solution for your risk level and budget.

Commercial Window Replacement

Old, damaged, or inefficient windows drive up energy costs and create an unwelcoming environment for employees and customers. We replace commercial windows of all types and sizes, from single-pane office windows to large-format picture windows in retail and industrial buildings. Our energy-efficient glass options include double-pane insulated glass units, low-E coatings that block UV rays and heat, and tinted glass that reduces glare and solar heat gain. In San Antonio's hot climate, upgrading to energy-efficient windows can significantly reduce your cooling costs and improve indoor comfort year-round. We handle the entire process from measurement to installation, ensuring a precise fit and clean finish every time.

Emergency Board-Up Services

When disaster strikes, you need a glass company that responds immediately. Infinity Glass Company offers 24/7 emergency board-up services for commercial properties throughout San Antonio. Whether your storefront was damaged by a vehicle, break-in, severe storm, or vandalism, we arrive fast to secure your building and prevent further damage. Our emergency team carries the materials needed to board up openings of any size, providing a secure temporary barrier until permanent glass can be installed. We coordinate with your insurance company and can schedule permanent replacement at your convenience, day or night.
