Professional Window Glass Replacement for San Antonio Homes

Your windows are one of the most critical components of your home's envelope. They keep conditioned air in, block outside noise, protect against intrusion, and contribute directly to your monthly energy costs. When window glass is cracked, shattered, or suffering from a failed seal, the problems compound quickly in San Antonio's climate. Summer temperatures regularly climb past 100 degrees, and a compromised window lets that heat pour into your living space while your air conditioner runs harder and longer trying to compensate.

Infinity Glass Company provides window glass replacement for homes throughout the San Antonio metropolitan area. We handle every type of residential window glass, from single-pane replacements in older homes to high-performance insulated glass units in modern construction. Our technicians measure every window on-site to ensure a precise fit, and we use quality materials rated for Texas heat, humidity, and severe weather.

Single-Pane Window Glass Replacement

Many older homes in San Antonio neighborhoods like Alamo Heights, Monte Vista, and the near-Westside still have original single-pane windows. While these windows have character, they provide virtually no insulation. Heat transfers directly through the glass, making rooms uncomfortable and driving up cooling costs. If a single-pane window cracks or breaks, replacement is straightforward. We measure the opening, cut new glass to size, and install it with proper glazing compound and weatherstripping to create a tight seal.

For homeowners on a budget who want to improve performance without replacing every window in the house, upgrading from single-pane to double-pane insulated glass is an option we can evaluate during our visit. If your existing window frames are structurally sound and in good condition, we may be able to retrofit new double-pane glass units into your current frames, giving you dramatically better insulation at a fraction of the cost of full window replacement.

Double-Pane and Insulated Glass Unit (IGU) Replacement

Most homes built or renovated after the 1990s have double-pane windows. These windows use two layers of glass separated by a sealed air or gas-filled space that acts as insulation. When both panes are intact and the seal is holding, double-pane windows do an excellent job of keeping heat out in summer and warmth in during the occasional San Antonio cold snap.

However, the seals on these units do fail over time, especially in climates with significant temperature swings. When that happens, moisture enters the space between the panes, causing the foggy or cloudy appearance you see from inside your home. This is not a cleaning problem. The moisture is trapped between the glass layers and cannot be wiped away. The only fix is to replace the insulated glass unit entirely.

We remove the failed glass unit and install a new one that matches your window's original specifications. Your existing frame, sash, and hardware stay in place. This approach typically costs 40 to 60 percent less than a full window replacement while restoring the energy efficiency and clarity your window had when it was new.

Tempered Safety Glass for Homes

Texas building code requires tempered safety glass in specific locations throughout your home. The Texas Residential Code mandates tempered glass for windows located within 18 inches of a door, windows in bathroom and shower areas, windows less than 18 inches above the floor, and any glass panel larger than 9 square feet that is close to walking areas. Tempered glass is heat-treated to be approximately four times stronger than standard annealed glass, and when it does break, it shatters into small, relatively harmless granular pieces instead of long, dangerous shards.

If a window in one of these code-required locations breaks, we install tempered glass to bring your home up to current safety standards. This is not optional in most cases, and it is an important safety measure that protects your family, especially children and pets.

Low-E Glass: Better Energy Efficiency for San Antonio Homes

Low-E, or low-emissivity, glass has a microscopically thin metallic coating that reflects infrared energy while still allowing visible light to pass through. In practical terms, Low-E glass blocks a significant portion of the solar heat that would normally enter your home through the windows. In San Antonio, where air conditioning is the single largest energy expense for most households, this translates to real savings on your electric bill, particularly during the five to six months of extreme heat we experience each year.

Low-E glass also blocks a large percentage of ultraviolet (UV) rays, which helps prevent fading on furniture, carpets, hardwood floors, and artwork near your windows. If you are replacing broken glass anyway, upgrading to Low-E glass is a cost-effective way to improve your home's performance without a major renovation project.

Our Window Glass Replacement Process

When you contact us about a window glass replacement, we start by gathering some basic information. If you can send a photo of the damaged window, that helps us prepare and often allows us to give you a ballpark estimate over the phone. We then schedule a visit to your home to take precise measurements of the window opening.

During the measurement visit, our technician examines the window frame, sash, and surrounding condition to determine whether the glass can be replaced independently or whether there are underlying issues with the frame that need to be addressed. This is particularly important for older homes where the wood frames may have moisture damage or the aluminum frames may have corrosion.

Once measurements are confirmed and you approve the quote, we fabricate or source the replacement glass and schedule your installation. Most installations are completed in a single visit. Our technician removes the old glass, cleans the frame, installs the new glass with proper sealing and glazing, and cleans up the work area before leaving.

For emergency situations where a window has been shattered by a storm, accident, or break-in, we can install temporary protective covering the same day and schedule the permanent glass replacement as quickly as possible.
