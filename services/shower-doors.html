Custom Shower Enclosures Designed for San Antonio Bathrooms

Your bathroom is one of the most-used spaces in your home, and the shower is its centerpiece. A well-designed shower enclosure does more than keep water off the floor. It defines the look of the entire room, makes the space feel larger and more open, and adds real value to your property. A poorly designed or outdated shower door, on the other hand, leaks, collects mold, and makes even a recently renovated bathroom feel dated.

Infinity Glass Company designs, fabricates, and installs custom shower enclosures for homes throughout San Antonio. We work with every style and configuration, from simple tub shower setups to large walk-in showers with multiple glass panels. Every project starts with a precise on-site measurement, and every installation is done by our experienced technicians using quality materials and hardware rated for daily use in San Antonio's humid climate.

Frameless Shower Doors

Frameless shower doors are the most popular choice for San Antonio homeowners renovating their bathrooms. These enclosures use thick tempered glass panels, typically 3/8 inch or 1/2 inch thick, that are held in place by small stainless steel or brass hinges and wall-mounted brackets. There is no metal frame around the edges of the glass, which creates a clean, open appearance that makes even a small bathroom feel larger.

The aesthetic benefit of frameless glass is obvious, but there are practical advantages as well. With no metal frame to trap water, soap scum, and mildew, frameless enclosures are significantly easier to clean and maintain. The hardware we use is corrosion-resistant and rated for tens of thousands of open-close cycles, which is important in San Antonio where high humidity accelerates wear on metal components.

Frameless designs work for a wide range of shower configurations: neo-angle showers in corner locations, large walk-in showers with a fixed panel and hinged door, alcove showers with a single sliding panel, and custom layouts that incorporate curved or angled glass. During our measurement visit, we evaluate your shower's specific conditions, including wall plumb, floor level, and tile placement, to recommend the frameless design that fits best.

Semi-Frameless Shower Doors

Semi-frameless shower doors offer a middle ground between the clean look of frameless glass and the structural reinforcement of a full frame. These enclosures use glass panels with metal framing around select edges, typically the top and sides, while leaving other edges frameless. The result is a modern appearance that costs less than fully frameless while still providing better aesthetics and easier maintenance than traditional framed doors.

Semi-frameless designs are particularly popular for tub shower enclosures and for homeowners who want an updated look without the higher price point of thick frameless glass panels. We offer semi-frameless options in chrome, brushed nickel, oil-rubbed bronze, and matte black finishes to match your existing bathroom fixtures.

Framed Shower Enclosures

Framed shower enclosures use a complete metal frame around all edges of each glass panel. This provides maximum structural support and is the most budget-friendly enclosure option. Modern framed enclosures look significantly better than the chrome-framed designs from 20 or 30 years ago. We offer framed doors in a wide range of finishes and profiles, from slim contemporary frames to wider traditional profiles.

Framed enclosures are a practical choice for rental properties, guest bathrooms, and situations where durability is the top priority. The frame creates a tight seal at all glass-to-wall and glass-to-glass joints, which some homeowners prefer for maximum water containment.

Hardware Options and Finishes

The hardware you choose for your shower enclosure has a significant impact on the final look. We offer hinges, handles, pulls, towel bars, and wall clamps in several popular finishes:

Brushed nickel is the most commonly selected finish in San Antonio homes because it resists fingerprints and water spots while complementing most bathroom fixture styles. Polished chrome provides a bright, reflective look that works well in contemporary and transitional bathrooms. Matte black has become increasingly popular for modern and farmhouse-style bathrooms, creating a bold contrast against white tile and clear glass. Oil-rubbed bronze offers a warm, traditional appearance that pairs well with natural stone and earth-tone tile. Polished brass provides a classic look that has seen a resurgence in high-end bathroom designs.

All of our standard hardware is manufactured from solid brass or stainless steel, not plated zinc or aluminum. This matters in a shower environment where moisture, heat, and daily use put constant stress on every component.

Glass Types for Shower Enclosures

All shower enclosure glass is tempered safety glass, which is approximately four times stronger than standard annealed glass and required by building code for shower applications. Beyond the standard clear glass, we offer several options:

Clear glass is the most popular choice because it shows off your tile work and makes the bathroom feel open. Low-iron (ultra-clear) glass eliminates the slight green tint that standard glass has at the edges, providing a crystal-clear appearance that is particularly noticeable on thick frameless panels. Frosted or rain glass provides privacy while still allowing light through, making it ideal for showers near windows or shared bathrooms. Reed and patterned glass options add texture and visual interest for a custom design touch.

We can also apply a protective coating to your shower glass that creates a hydrophobic surface, causing water to bead and roll off instead of clinging to the glass. This significantly reduces water spot and mineral buildup, which is a real benefit in San Antonio where our water has high mineral content.

Our Shower Door Installation Process

Every shower enclosure project begins with a visit to your home. We do not rely on rough measurements from contractor plans or measurements you take yourself, because shower walls are rarely perfectly plumb, floors are rarely perfectly level, and tile surfaces have variations that affect how glass panels need to be cut and positioned.

Our technician measures every surface condition of your shower or tub area, discusses design options and hardware finishes with you, and documents everything needed to fabricate your glass panels accurately. After the measurement, we provide a written quote with pricing, timeline, and material details.

Once you approve the quote, your glass panels are fabricated and the hardware is prepared. This typically takes 5 to 10 business days. On installation day, our technician removes any existing enclosure if needed, prepares the mounting surfaces, installs the glass panels, secures all hardware, applies sealant where needed, and tests the door operation. We clean up thoroughly and walk you through the finished enclosure before we leave.

Custom shower door costs in San Antonio vary based on the design, glass type, and hardware you choose. Semi-frameless options are generally less expensive, starting around $500 to $1,000. We provide detailed quotes after measuring your specific shower opening so you have an exact price before committing.
Frameless shower doors use thick tempered glass panels held in place by minimal metal hardware, with no metal frame around the glass edges. This creates a clean, open look and makes the shower easier to clean since there is less hardware and fewer crevices where mold can grow. Semi-frameless doors combine glass panels with a partial metal frame, typically around the fixed panel or the door edges. They offer a similar modern aesthetic to frameless at a lower cost, with added structural support from the frame components.
