Types of Storefront Glass We Install

We work with a wide range of glass types to match your business's aesthetic, performance, and budget requirements:

Clear Plate Glass: Traditional plate glass offers maximum transparency and a clean, classic look. It is the most common choice for retail storefronts, restaurant windows, and display facades. Plate glass provides excellent clarity and allows natural light to flood your interior space, creating an open, inviting atmosphere for customers.

Tempered Safety Glass: Tempered glass is heat-treated to be approximately four times stronger than standard annealed glass. When it does break, it shatters into small, rounded pieces rather than sharp shards, reducing the risk of injury. Most commercial building codes in San Antonio require tempered glass for storefront applications, and we ensure every installation meets those requirements.

Laminated Glass: Laminated glass consists of two or more layers of glass bonded together with a clear interlayer, typically made of polyvinyl butyral (PVB). If the glass breaks, the interlayer holds the fragments in place, maintaining the barrier and deterring break-ins. Laminated storefront glass is an excellent choice for businesses that need added security without sacrificing visibility.

Tinted Glass: Tinted glass reduces solar heat gain, blocks UV rays, and adds a layer of privacy during daytime hours. Available in bronze, gray, green, and blue tints, this option enhances your storefront's appearance while improving energy efficiency and protecting interior furnishings from sun damage.

Low-E Glass: Low-emissivity (low-E) glass features a microscopically thin metallic coating that reflects infrared heat while allowing visible light to pass through. In San Antonio's hot climate, low-E storefront glass can significantly reduce cooling costs, making it one of the smartest investments for energy-conscious business owners.

Frosted and Textured Glass: For businesses that need privacy without sacrificing light, frosted and textured glass options diffuse visibility while maintaining an open feel. These are popular choices for medical offices, salons, law firms, and professional service providers in San Antonio.

Storefront Entry Systems

The entry system is the centerpiece of your storefront. We install and service a complete range of commercial entry systems to suit different business types and traffic volumes:

Aluminum-Framed Storefront Systems: The most widely used commercial storefront configuration in San Antonio. Aluminum frames are lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and available in clear anodized, bronze, black, and custom powder-coated finishes. These systems accommodate single-pane and insulated glass units, giving you flexibility in glass selection.

Frameless Glass Entry Systems: For a sleek, modern appearance, frameless storefront systems use heavy-duty tempered or laminated glass panels held in place by minimal hardware. Frameless designs create an unobstructed view and a premium aesthetic that works particularly well for boutiques, galleries, upscale restaurants, and professional offices.

Automatic Sliding Doors: We install and service automatic sliding door systems that provide convenient, hands-free entry for customers. These systems are essential for ADA compliance, high-traffic retail locations, grocery stores, and medical facilities throughout San Antonio.

Double Door Entries: Classic double-door glass entry systems offer a balanced, symmetrical appearance. We install these with premium hardware including closers, panic bars, pull handles, and commercial-grade locksets that stand up to heavy daily use.

ADA-Compliant Glass Entry Systems

Accessibility matters. San Antonio businesses are required to meet Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) standards for their storefront entry systems. We ensure that every glass entry we install complies with ADA requirements, including proper door width, threshold height, hardware placement, and automatic door operation where required. Our team stays current on both federal ADA guidelines and local San Antonio building code requirements so you can be confident your storefront meets all applicable standards.

Energy-Efficient Storefront Glass

San Antonio's long, hot summers make energy efficiency a major concern for commercial property owners. Single-pane storefront glass is one of the biggest sources of energy loss in commercial buildings, allowing heat to pour in during summer and conditioned air to escape year-round. Upgrading to insulated glass units (IGUs), low-E coated glass, or tinted glass can dramatically reduce your HVAC costs and improve comfort for employees and customers inside your building.

We help San Antonio business owners choose the right energy-efficient glass for their specific needs and budget. Many of our clients see a noticeable reduction in their utility bills after upgrading their storefront glass, and the improved comfort makes the investment worthwhile even before you factor in the energy savings.
