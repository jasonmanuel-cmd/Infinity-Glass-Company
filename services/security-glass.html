Protect Your San Antonio Property with Security Glass

Security is a top priority for businesses and homeowners in San Antonio. Break-ins, vandalism, severe storms, and accidental impacts can all cause significant damage and create safety hazards when standard glass is involved. Security glass solutions provide a stronger, more resilient barrier that protects your property, your people, and your investment without sacrificing the visibility and natural light that glass provides.

Infinity Glass Company installs a comprehensive range of security glass products for commercial and residential properties throughout San Antonio, Texas. From laminated glass for storefronts to bullet-resistant glazing for high-security applications, our team helps you choose the right level of protection for your specific needs and budget.

Laminated Security Glass

Laminated glass is one of the most versatile and widely used security glass products available. It consists of two or more layers of glass bonded together with a clear polyvinyl butyral (PVB) interlayer under heat and pressure. When the glass is impacted, the interlayer holds the fragments in place, maintaining the barrier and preventing easy penetration.

Laminated security glass is the standard choice for storefront windows, display cases, skylights, and any application where break-in resistance and occupant safety are priorities. Unlike tempered glass, which shatters completely upon impact, laminated glass stays intact even when cracked, making it significantly harder for an intruder to gain entry. The PVB interlayer also blocks up to 99% of UV radiation, protecting interior furnishings, merchandise, and flooring from sun damage.

We offer laminated glass in various thicknesses, from standard 1/8-inch configurations to heavy-duty security-grade panels that meet specific UL and ASTM standards. For San Antonio businesses that need enhanced security without the appearance of fortified construction, laminated glass looks identical to standard glass while providing a much higher level of protection.

Tempered Safety Glass

Tempered glass is heat-treated to be approximately four times stronger than standard annealed glass. It is the most common safety glass product used in commercial and residential applications. When tempered glass does break, it shatters into small, rounded granules rather than long, sharp shards, dramatically reducing the risk of cuts and serious injury.

Building codes throughout San Antonio and the state of Texas require tempered glass in specific locations, including bathroom windows, glass panels near doors, sidelights, storefront windows at ground level, and any glass surface where human impact is a concern. We install tempered glass that meets all applicable building code requirements, and we can help you determine where tempered glass is required in your building.

While tempered glass is not a security product in the same way as laminated glass, its increased strength makes it more resistant to impact from everyday hazards such as flying debris, accidental bumps, and minor collisions. For many San Antonio businesses and homes, tempered glass provides the right combination of safety, strength, and cost-effectiveness.

Polycarbonate and Multi-Layer Security Panels

For applications that require maximum impact resistance, we install polycarbonate and multi-layer security panels. Polycarbonate is a thermoplastic material that is virtually unbreakable, offering impact resistance many times greater than glass. It is lightweight, optically clear, and can be used alone or in combination with glass layers for enhanced protection.

Multi-layer security panels combine glass and polycarbonate or acrylic layers to achieve specific performance requirements. These systems are used in high-security environments where both optical clarity and extreme impact resistance are needed, such as government buildings, detention facilities, and military installations.

Ballistic-Rated and Bullet-Resistant Glass

For San Antonio businesses that require the highest level of glass security, we install bullet-resistant glazing systems rated to specific UL Protection Levels. These systems are commonly used in banks, credit unions, jewelry stores, pharmacies, government offices, and any commercial property where ballistic protection is a concern.

Bullet-resistant glass is constructed from multiple layers of glass and polycarbonate, engineered to absorb and disperse the energy of a ballistic impact. Different UL protection levels offer varying degrees of resistance, from handgun rounds to high-powered rifle ammunition. Our team can help you determine the appropriate UL rating for your application and install a system that meets your security requirements while maintaining acceptable optical clarity and light transmission.

Hurricane-Rated and Impact-Resistant Glass

While San Antonio sits inland from the Gulf Coast, the city regularly experiences severe thunderstorms, high winds, tornadoes, and the outer bands of hurricanes that move into South Texas. Commercial buildings with large glass facades are particularly vulnerable to wind-borne debris during these events.

We install hurricane-rated and impact-resistant glass systems that meet Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) standards and Florida Building Code requirements, which are among the most stringent in the nation. These systems use laminated glass that remains intact even when impacted by flying debris, preventing dangerous glass fragments from entering the building and maintaining the building envelope during a severe weather event.

Impact-resistant glass also provides year-round benefits beyond storm protection, including improved security against break-ins, reduced outside noise, and enhanced UV protection. For San Antonio commercial property owners, hurricane-rated glass is a smart investment that provides multiple layers of value.
