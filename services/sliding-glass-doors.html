Sliding Glass Door Repair and Replacement for San Antonio Homes

Sliding glass doors are one of the most heavily used features in San Antonio homes. They connect your indoor living space to the backyard, let in natural light, and provide ventilation when the weather is pleasant. But years of daily use combined with San Antonio's hot, dusty climate take a real toll on the rollers, tracks, weather stripping, and glass panels that make these doors function properly.

When a sliding glass door starts sticking, grinding, or refusing to close all the way, it is more than an inconvenience. A sliding door that does not close and lock properly is a security vulnerability, an energy efficiency problem that lets conditioned air escape, and a safety concern for households with children or pets. Infinity Glass Company repairs and replaces sliding glass door components for homes throughout San Antonio, restoring doors to smooth, secure operation without the expense of replacing the entire door system.

Sliding Door Glass Panel Replacement

The most common sliding door issue we handle is broken glass. Sliding door panels are large, thin, and exposed, which makes them vulnerable to impact from lawn equipment, hail, stray sports balls, and accidental contact. When a sliding door glass panel breaks, it compromises your home's security and energy efficiency immediately, and it leaves your living space exposed to insects, rain, and outdoor temperatures.

We replace sliding door glass panels by measuring the existing panel, fabricating a new piece of tempered or insulated glass to match, and swapping the panels in the existing frame. In most cases, we can use the same frame and track system, which keeps the cost significantly lower than a full door replacement. We match the glass type to what was originally installed, whether that is single-pane tempered glass, double-pane insulated glass, or Low-E coated glass for improved energy performance.

If your sliding door has been broken for a while and the original glass type is no longer available, we recommend upgrading to a double-pane insulated glass unit. This is a common upgrade in San Antonio homes because it provides much better insulation against our extreme summer heat while fitting into the existing door frame.

Roller and Track Repair

If your sliding door is hard to push open, requires excessive force, sticks at certain points along the track, or has come off the track entirely, the problem is almost always related to the rollers or the track itself. Sliding door rollers wear out over time as the bearings dry out, the wheel surfaces flatten, and the accumulated dirt and debris in the track creates friction.

Our sliding door repair process starts by removing the door panel from the track. We clean the entire track thoroughly, removing years of accumulated dirt, pet hair, and debris. We then inspect and replace the rollers, installing new bearing assemblies rated for the weight of your specific door panel. After replacing the rollers, we adjust the door's alignment so it sits properly on the track, rolls smoothly with minimal effort, and engages the lock mechanism securely.

For doors that have come completely off the track, we realign the panel, replace any damaged track components, and adjust the roller height to prevent the door from jumping the track again. This is a common issue in San Antonio homes where doors are opened and closed dozens of times per day, especially during the months when families spend most of their time in the backyard.

Weather Stripping and Sealing

San Antonio's heat makes weather stripping a critical component of your sliding door. The foam, rubber, or pile seals around the edges of your sliding door panels are designed to block air infiltration and keep insects out. Over time, these seals compress, crack, and pull away from the frame, allowing conditioned air to escape and outdoor air to enter.

Replacing weather stripping is an inexpensive repair that makes a noticeable difference in your home's comfort and energy costs. We replace all the seals around your sliding door panels, including the interlock seals where the two panels meet, the perimeter seals around each panel, and the bottom sweep that seals the gap between the door and the track. New weather stripping also helps your door close and lock more easily because the panels align properly with fresh, properly compressed seals.

Handle, Lock, and Hardware Replacement

The handle and lock on your sliding glass door take constant physical stress every time the door is opened and closed. Handles loosen from their mounting screws, lock mechanisms wear out, and the strike plates on the door frame become bent or misaligned. A sliding door with a broken or loose lock is a security risk, and many San Antonio homeowners are surprised to find that their patio door has not been locking properly for months.

We replace sliding door handles, mortise locks, hook locks, and security pins with quality hardware that matches your door's existing hole pattern or provides an upgraded fit. For homeowners who want improved security, we can install auxiliary foot locks, security bars, or upgraded multi-point locking systems that provide additional protection beyond the standard latch.

When to Repair vs. Replace

Not every sliding door problem requires a full door replacement. In many cases, a combination of new glass, fresh rollers, cleaned tracks, new weather stripping, and updated hardware can restore a 15 to 20-year-old door to smooth, reliable operation at a fraction of the cost of a new door system.

We recommend full door replacement when the aluminum or vinyl frame has significant corrosion or structural damage, when the door track is severely worn or bent beyond repair, or when you want to change the size or configuration of the door opening. During our visit, we evaluate the condition of your complete door system and recommend the approach that gives you the best value for your situation.
