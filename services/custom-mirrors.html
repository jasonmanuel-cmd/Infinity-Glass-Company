Custom Mirror Design and Installation for San Antonio Homes

Mirrors are one of the most versatile design elements in a home. A well-placed mirror can make a small bathroom feel twice as large, bounce natural light deeper into a dark hallway, serve as a practical daily-use surface in your bedroom or dressing area, and function as a decorative focal point that ties a room together. Unlike many home improvement projects, custom mirrors deliver an immediate visual impact without the cost or disruption of a full renovation.

Infinity Glass Company fabricates and installs custom mirrors for homes throughout San Antonio. We cut mirrors to your exact dimensions, offer several edge treatments and glass options, and mount every mirror with professional-grade hardware designed for your wall type. Whether you need a single bathroom vanity mirror or a floor-to-ceiling mirrored wall, we handle the entire process from measurement to final installation.

Bathroom Vanity Mirrors

The bathroom mirror is the most frequently used mirror in most homes, and replacing a dated or damaged vanity mirror is one of the simplest upgrades you can make to refresh a bathroom. We fabricate bathroom mirrors in any size, from small powder room mirrors to wide double-vanity mirrors that span the entire wall above a double-sink cabinet.

For San Antonio homeowners replacing an existing vanity mirror, we can cut a new mirror to match the exact dimensions of the old one, or update the size and shape to complement a renovation. Popular options include large rectangular mirrors with polished edges for a clean, contemporary look, mirrors with beveled edges that add a decorative border, and rounded or arched mirrors that soften the lines of a bathroom.

We also install mirrors cut to fit precisely between wall-mounted light fixtures or around medicine cabinets. During our measurement visit, we evaluate your wall conditions and discuss mounting options to ensure a secure, level installation that matches your bathroom design.

Full-Length Closet Mirrors

A full-length mirror is essential in a bedroom or dressing area, and the closet door is the most practical location for one. We install full-length mirror panels on existing sliding closet doors, fabricate new mirrored door panels to replace solid doors, and mount standalone full-length mirrors on walls adjacent to closet openings.

For closet door mirrors, we measure the exact dimensions of each door panel and cut mirror glass to fit within the door frame or track system. The mirror is securely attached to the door surface with mirror clips or adhesive, depending on the door construction. This gives you a full-length reflection without taking up any additional wall or floor space, which is particularly valuable in San Antonio bedrooms where space is often at a premium.

Full-Wall and Floor-to-Ceiling Mirrors

Full-wall mirrors create a dramatic visual effect by reflecting the entire room, effectively doubling the perceived depth of the space. This is a popular design choice for dining rooms, living areas, home gyms, and narrow hallways. Floor-to-ceiling mirrors serve a similar purpose and are commonly installed in fitness areas, dance spaces, and dressing rooms.

Large mirror installations require careful planning and professional mounting. Panels become very heavy as they increase in size, so we evaluate your wall construction, locate studs, and use the appropriate combination of adhesive and mechanical fasteners to ensure a secure installation. For walls wider than a single mirror panel, we install multiple panels with tight, clean seams that minimize the visual break between panels.

Many San Antonio homeowners with older homes in neighborhoods like Alamo Heights and Monte Vista use full-wall mirrors to brighten rooms that have limited natural light. The mirror surface reflects existing light sources, making spaces feel brighter and more open without adding fixtures or windows.

Beveled Edge and Decorative Mirrors

A beveled edge transforms a plain mirror into a decorative element. The bevel is a strip ground and polished at an angle along the mirror's perimeter, typically ranging from 1/2 inch to 2 inches wide. This angled edge catches and refracts light, creating a subtle sparkle that frames the reflection and adds a sense of depth and quality.

Beveled mirrors work particularly well in formal spaces like dining rooms, entryways, and living rooms. They pair naturally with traditional and transitional decor styles and are available in any shape, including rectangular, oval, round, arched, and custom geometric forms. We offer bevel widths from narrow 1/2-inch accents to wide 2-inch borders for a more pronounced effect.

Beyond beveled edges, we fabricate decorative mirrors in a variety of configurations: antique-finish mirrors with a weathered, aged patina; smoked grey mirrors that create a moodier, more subdued reflection; and tinted mirrors in bronze or champagne tones that complement warm color palettes. These specialty glass options allow you to create a mirror that serves as a design statement rather than just a functional surface.

Mirrored Accent Walls

A mirrored accent wall is a single wall covered in mirror panels, typically installed behind a bed, along a dining room wall, or in a bar or entertainment area. This design technique adds depth, light, and visual interest to a room while creating a focal point that anchors the space.

We work with San Antonio homeowners and interior designers to plan mirrored accent walls that complement the room's existing design. Panel layout, seam placement, edge treatments, and glass tint all factor into the final result. We handle the measurement, fabrication, and installation, working around electrical outlets, switches, and decorative elements as needed.

Mirror Types and Glass Options

Standard silver-backed mirrors provide the bright, true-to-life reflection most people expect in a bathroom or bedroom. We also offer several specialty options. Grey-tinted mirrors reduce brightness slightly and work well in rooms with strong natural light. Bronze-tinted mirrors cast a warm tone that complements earth-tone color schemes common in San Antonio homes. Antique mirrors have a distressed backing that creates an aged, vintage appearance suitable for decorative and accent applications.

All of our mirrors use quality glass with consistent reflective coatings that produce clear, distortion-free images across the entire surface. We source our mirror glass from established suppliers and inspect each piece before installation to ensure it meets our quality standards.
