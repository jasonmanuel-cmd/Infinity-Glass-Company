Custom Glass Tabletops and Shelves for San Antonio Homes

A glass tabletop is one of the simplest and most effective ways to protect your furniture while showing off the wood, stone, or metal surface underneath. Whether you have a dining table that needs protection from daily use, a coffee table you want to preserve, or a desk that needs a durable writing surface, a custom-cut glass top provides a clean, functional layer that keeps your furniture looking new.

Infinity Glass Company fabricates and installs custom glass tabletops and shelves for homes throughout San Antonio. We cut glass to any shape and size, offer several thickness and edge treatment options, and deliver or install every piece with the precision fit your furniture requires.

Custom Glass Tabletops

Every glass tabletop we produce starts with accurate measurements of your existing furniture. We can create glass tops to match the exact shape of your table, whether it is a standard rectangle, a round pedestal table, an oval dining table, a triangle accent table, or an irregular freeform shape. For tables with unusual contours, we create a template that captures every curve and corner, ensuring a glass top that follows the table edge precisely with a consistent overhang or inset, depending on your preference.

Tempered glass is our standard recommendation for all tabletop applications. Tempered glass is heat-treated during manufacturing to be approximately four times stronger than standard annealed glass. When it does break, it shatters into small, relatively harmless granular pieces instead of long, sharp shards. This is particularly important for dining tables and coffee tables where family members, especially children, interact with the glass surface daily.

For dining tables, we typically recommend 1/2-inch thick glass for spans over 24 inches and 3/8-inch thick glass for smaller surfaces. Coffee table glass is commonly 3/8 inch or 1/4 inch thick depending on the size of the table and how much weight the glass will need to support. During our consultation, we recommend the appropriate thickness based on your table dimensions and intended use.

Edge Treatments

The edge treatment you choose affects both the appearance and safety of your glass tabletop. Flat polished edges are squared off and smooth, providing a clean, contemporary look that works well with modern furniture. Beveled edges are ground at an angle along the perimeter, creating a decorative border that catches light and adds a more finished, elegant appearance. Pencil-polished edges are rounded to a smooth, bullnose profile that eliminates any sharp corners, making them a popular choice for households with young children or pets.

All of our edge treatments are performed in-house by our fabrication team, which means we control the quality and finish of every piece. We do not send glass out to a third-party fabricator, so the edge quality on your tabletop is consistent from one end to the other.

Glass Shelves and Display Surfaces

Tempered glass shelves provide clean, modern storage and display space for virtually any room in your home. We fabricate shelves for bathroom vanities, kitchen cabinets, linen closets, display cabinets, shower niches, and floating wall-mounted configurations. Each shelf is cut to fit the exact dimensions of the space, with tight tolerances that ensure a clean, professional appearance.

Floating glass shelves, mounted with concealed bracket systems, create the appearance of a glass surface suspended from the wall without visible supports. These are popular in living rooms, bedrooms, and bathrooms for displaying decorative items, books, or toiletries. We install floating shelves using hardware rated for the weight capacity your application requires.

For bathroom and shower applications, we recommend tempered glass shelves that can handle the temperature and moisture conditions. Clear glass shows off tile work behind the shelf, while frosted glass provides a softer look that hides water spots more effectively.

Glass Thickness Options

We stock and fabricate glass in several standard thicknesses. 1/4-inch glass works well for small shelf applications, cabinet inserts, and lightweight tabletop overlays where the glass sits flat on a supporting surface. 3/8-inch glass is our most common thickness for tabletops and provides good rigidity for medium-span surfaces. 1/2-inch glass offers maximum rigidity and a substantial, premium feel for large dining tables and heavy-use surfaces. We also work with 5/8-inch and 3/4-inch glass for specialty applications that require additional strength or a thicker visual profile.
