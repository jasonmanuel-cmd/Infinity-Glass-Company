Emergency Board-Up Services

When permanent glass replacement cannot be completed immediately, we provide professional board-up services to secure your property. Our emergency team carries plywood, polycarbonate panels, and the fasteners needed to board up openings of any size, from a single broken window to an entire storefront facade.

Board-up service is essential after break-ins, vehicle impacts, severe storms, and any situation where a large glass opening has been compromised. A proper board-up prevents rain, wind, animals, and unauthorized individuals from entering your property while permanent glass is fabricated and installed. We also remove and dispose of broken glass shards safely, eliminating the immediate injury hazard from scattered debris.

Storm and Weather Damage Glass Repair

San Antonio experiences a wide range of severe weather events that can damage glass, including hailstorms, high winds, tornadoes, and the remnants of Gulf Coast hurricanes. Storm damage to glass is often widespread, affecting multiple windows or an entire building facade at once.

Our emergency storm damage response team is equipped to handle large-scale glass repair projects. We prioritize commercial properties that have sustained extensive damage, helping business owners secure their buildings and begin the restoration process quickly. We document all damage for insurance purposes and coordinate with insurance adjusters to streamline your claim.
