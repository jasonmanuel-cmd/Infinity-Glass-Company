San Antonio Glass Experts

Your Trusted Glass Partner in San Antonio

Infinity Glass Company is proud to serve the San Antonio metropolitan area with expert residential and commercial glass solutions. As one of the largest and fastest-growing cities in Texas, San Antonio presents unique glass needs across its diverse neighborhoods, from the historic charm of Alamo Heights and King William to the modern developments in Stone Oak and The Dominion.

San Antonio's climate creates specific challenges for glass installations. The intense summer heat, with temperatures regularly exceeding 100°F, puts stress on window seals and can cause double-pane insulated glass units (IGUs) to fail, resulting in foggy or cloudy windows. Our high UV exposure accelerates the degradation of older window films and sealants. Additionally, the region is prone to severe thunderstorms with hail and high winds, which can cause impact damage to residential and commercial windows alike.

We understand these local conditions because we live and work here. Our team recommends energy-efficient glass solutions that help San Antonio homeowners and businesses reduce cooling costs while maintaining comfort. Whether you need to replace a heat-damaged foggy window, upgrade to low-E glass, or install impact-resistant windows before storm season, we have the expertise and materials to get the job done right.

Neighborhoods We Serve

Glass Services Across San Antonio

We provide comprehensive glass services throughout San Antonio's most established and growing neighborhoods.

Alamo Heights & Terrell Hills

These premier neighborhoods feature a mix of historic mid-century homes and custom-built residences. Older window frames often need careful glass replacement that matches original specifications. We specialize in fitting new glass into existing frames, preserving the character of these established homes while improving energy efficiency.

Stone Oak & The Dominion

North San Antonio's upscale communities are known for large-format windows, open floor plans, and modern glass features. Homeowners here frequently request custom shower enclosures, expansive picture windows, and decorative glass elements. The Dominion's gated communities and luxury properties often require coordinated installations that meet HOA standards.

King William & Southtown

San Antonio's historic South Side features beautifully restored Victorian and Craftsman-era homes. Glass work in these neighborhoods demands sensitivity to period-appropriate aesthetics. We offer custom solutions that honor the architectural heritage while introducing modern performance, including restoration-grade window glass and period-appropriate mirror work.

Helotes & Far West Side

The rapidly growing communities along the Bandera Road corridor include a mix of new construction and established ranch-style homes. New builds often feature contemporary glass packages, while older homes may need full window replacements to improve insulation against the Hill Country's temperature swings.

North Central San Antonio & I-35 Corridor

Commercial developments along the I-35 corridor and in the North Central area include retail centers, office parks, and mixed-use properties. We provide storefront glass, office partitions, and commercial window replacement for businesses throughout this thriving commercial zone.
